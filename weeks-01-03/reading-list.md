# Weeks 1–3 Reading List

Tags: **[required]** — assigned before a lecture, assumed in class · **[recommended]**
— strengthens the lecture · **[gap-filler]** — for students with thin ML background;
skimmable by the ML-literate.

URLs verified 2026-07-14 except where flagged.

## For Lecture 02 (assigned at L01)

- **[required]** Karpathy, *[1hr Talk] Intro to Large Language Models* (YouTube, 2023)
  — <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zjkBMFhNj_g>. The designated gap-filler for the
  whole unit; ML-literate students may skim.
- **[recommended]** 3Blue1Brown, *Transformers, the tech behind LLMs* (Deep Learning
  ch. 5) — <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wjZofJX0v4M> — and its attention chapter
  (ch. 6, same playlist ⚠ verify direct link before publishing). Visual-first
  treatment of what L01 waved hands at.
- **[gap-filler]** Karpathy, *Deep Dive into LLMs like ChatGPT* (YouTube, 2025) —
  <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI>. Long; for the genuinely curious.
- **[gap-filler]** Vaswani et al., *Attention Is All You Need* (NeurIPS 2017) —
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762>. Skim §1–2 for the historical anchor.
- **[gap-filler]** Ouyang et al., *Training language models to follow instructions
  with human feedback* (2022) — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155>. Why assistants
  answer instead of complete.

## For Lecture 03 (assigned at L02)

- **[required]** Anthropic, *Building Effective Agents* (Dec 2024) —
  <https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents>. The unit's core
  essay: workflows vs agents; simplest-thing-that-works.
- **[required]** Anthropic API docs, *Tool use* — skim now, depth at L05.
- **[recommended]** Yao et al., *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language
  Models* (ICLR 2023) — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629>. §1–3.
